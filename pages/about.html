Who I Am

An Engineer Committed to Quality

I'm Muhammad Amir — a Full-Stack Software Engineer with over 2 years of hands-on experience designing, developing, and deploying robust applications across the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

My expertise lies deeply in JavaScript ES6+, React, Node.js, and Express.js, combined with MongoDB for flexible data architecture. I have a proven track record of independently architecting end-to-end systems, from secure authentication logic to role-gated dashboards and cloud deployments on platforms like Vercel and Railway.

Beyond traditional web development, I hold a complementary passion for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. By integrating technologies like FastAPI, FAISS, and NLP models, I actively build applications that leverage vector search and LLMs to solve complex, modern problems efficiently.
